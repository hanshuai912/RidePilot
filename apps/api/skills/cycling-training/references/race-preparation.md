# Cycling race preparation

Adapted from the pinned `jelmervdm` event prioritization and `mprecilio20` race-execution references. See `../UPSTREAM.md`.

- Use only the supplied race date, distance, priority, status and current plan. An A race can justify more recovery and a taper; a B race may need a smaller reduction; a C race may be treated as part of training. The exact taper depends on event distance and known training history, so do not force an upstream percentage or TSB target when those data are absent.
- A nearby important race favors reducing fatigue, retaining a small amount of familiar race-relevant work and protecting recovery. Do not promise last-minute fitness gains. A changed or cancelled race must be replanned from the current valid calendar, never by blindly restoring an old plan.
- Do not place a workout on a confirmed race day or an existing assessment day. If multiple events compete, explain the priority and what the proposed workouts give up.
- The API currently has no course elevation, terrain, weather, HRV or load trend in this scene. Do not invent route-specific pacing or claim that NP, IF, TSS or race-day freshness has been measured.
- This scene changes only future training workouts through a reviewable proposal. Do not output nutrition, fueling, hydration or supplement advice, and do not claim to modify the race event itself.
