# Race Day & Event Fueling Protocols

Event nutrition begins days prior to the start gun and extends into the immediate post-race recovery window.

> [!NOTE]
> **Educational Disclaimer**: Race-day fueling plans must be tested during training sessions. Never try new foods, gels, or electrolyte products on race day.

---

## 1. Pre-Race Carbohydrate Loading (24–48 Hours Out)

Carbohydrate loading supersaturates muscle glycogen stores (increasing muscle glycogen from normal ~150 mmol/kg to ~200–250 mmol/kg).

- **Target Intake**: **10 – 12 g/kg body mass / day** for 36–48 hours prior to endurance events lasting longer than 90 minutes.
- **Dietary Composition**: Focus on low-fiber, low-fat, high-glycemic carbohydrates (white rice, pasta, bagels, applesauce, fruit juice, sports drinks) to prevent gastrointestinal heaviness and distress.

---

## 2. Pre-Race Meal (1–4 Hours Before Start)

| Timing Before Start | Carbohydrate Target | Meal Guidelines & Examples |
| ------------------- | ------------------- | -------------------------- |
| **3 – 4 hours out** | **3.0 – 4.0 g/kg** | Complete meal: Oatmeal with syrup, banana, white toast with jelly, 500ml sodium fluid |
| **2 hours out** | **2.0 g/kg** | Light meal: Bagel with honey, sports drink |
| **1 hour out** | **1.0 g/kg** | Liquid / Gel: Hydrogel liquid or gel + 300ml water |
| **15 minutes out** | **25 – 30 g** | 1 energy gel taken with 150ml water at start line |

---

## 3. Hourly Intra-Race Execution Template

During long endurance events (marathon, triathlon, cycling races):

```
Hour 1: 60g CHO + 500ml Fluid + 400mg Sodium
Hour 2: 75g CHO + 600ml Fluid + 500mg Sodium
Hour 3: 75g CHO + 600ml Fluid + 500mg Sodium
Hour 4+: 75-90g CHO (dual-transporter) + 600-750ml Fluid + 600mg Sodium
```

---

## 4. Post-Race Recovery Window (0–4 Hours Post-Event)

Rapid glycogen resynthesis and muscle protein repair require immediate carbohydrate and protein intake:

- **Carbohydrate Co-Ingestion**: **1.0 – 1.2 g/kg/hour** of high-GI carbohydrates for the first 4 hours post-race.
- **Protein Co-Ingestion**: Add **0.3 g/kg** protein to each hourly recovery snack to accelerate muscle tissue repair and glycogen resynthesis rates.
- **Rehydration Target**: Consume **150%** of total mass lost during the event over the subsequent 4–6 hours, incorporating sodium to maintain fluid retention.
