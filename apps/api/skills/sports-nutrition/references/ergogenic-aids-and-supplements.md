# Ergogenic Aids & Evidence-Based Supplementation

Supplements should only be evaluated after daily energy, macronutrients, and sleep foundations are established. This guide categorizes supplements using the Australian Institute of Sport (AIS) & ISSN evidence frameworks.

> [!NOTE]
> **Educational & Safety Disclaimer**: Supplement safety and purity are paramount. Athletes must ensure all supplements are third-party tested (e.g. Informed-Sport, NSF Certified for Sport) to prevent contamination with banned substances. Consult a medical professional before starting any supplement regimen.

---

## 1. AIS Category A Supplements (Strong Scientific Evidence)

| Supplement | Recommended Dosage | Optimal Timing | Physiological Mechanism | Target Sports |
| ---------- | ------------------ | -------------- | ----------------------- | ------------- |
| **Creatine Monohydrate** | 3–5 g/day (or 20 g/day for 5 days loading) | Post-workout with carbohydrates | Increases intramuscular phosphocreatine (PCr) stores | Strength, sprinting, track cycling, repeated high-intensity efforts |
| **Caffeine** | 3–6 mg/kg body mass | 45–60 minutes pre-exercise (or 1–2 mg/kg in late race) | Adenosine receptor antagonist, reduces rating of perceived exertion (RPE) | All endurance, team, and high-intensity sports |
| **Beta-Alanine** | 3.2–6.4 g/day for 4–8 weeks | Daily split doses (to avoid paresthesia) | Increases muscle carnosine stores; buffers intracellular $\text{H}^+$ ions | High-intensity efforts lasting 1–10 minutes (rowing, 800m-1500m run, track cycling) |
| **Dietary Nitrates (Beetroot Juice)** | 300–600 mg nitrate (~400ml juice) | 2–3 hours pre-exercise | Converted to Nitric Oxide ($\text{NO}$); lowers $\text{O}_2$ cost of submaximal exercise | Cycling, running, rowing, cross-country skiing |
| **Sodium Bicarbonate** | 0.2–0.4 g/kg body mass | 90–120 minutes pre-exercise with carbs | Extracellular buffering of hydrogen ions; increases blood pH | Anaerobic capacity events (1–7 minutes duration) |

---

## 2. AIS Category B Supplements (Emerging / Context-Specific Evidence)

- **Collagen & Vitamin C**: 10–15g hydrolysed collagen + 50mg Vitamin C taken 30–60 minutes prior to tendon loading/rehab to stimulate collagen synthesis in connective tissues.
- **Curcumin & Tart Cherry Juice**: High-polyphenol anti-inflammatory compounds to accelerate short-term recovery during multi-day tournament play (avoid during adaptation phases as blunting ROS can blunt mitochondrial adaptations).
- **Probiotics**: Daily multi-strain probiotic supplementation during heavy winter volume to support gut mucosa barrier function and upper respiratory tract infection (URTI) resistance.

---

## 3. Supplement Safety & Anti-Doping Protocols

- **Third-Party Certification**: Athletes subject to WADA anti-doping regulations must select products carrying **Informed-Sport**, **NSF Certified for Sport**, or **HASTA** certification badges.
- **Micro-Dosing & Gastrointestinal Tolerability**: High doses of sodium bicarbonate or caffeine can trigger severe gastrointestinal distress or tachycardia; always assess individual tolerance during non-critical training sessions.
