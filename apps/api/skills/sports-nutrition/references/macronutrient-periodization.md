# Macronutrient Periodization for Athletes

Macronutrient periodization involves purposefully altering daily carbohydrate, protein, and fat intakes in alignment with training volume, workout intensity, and periodization cycles (e.g. general preparation, high-intensity intervals, taper, competition, or off-season recovery).

> [!NOTE]
> **Educational Disclaimer**: This reference is for informational and educational purposes only. Individual requirements vary based on metabolism, training load, and health status.

---

## 1. Energy Expenditure & Baseline TDEE Calculations

Daily energy recommendations must start from an athlete's biological baseline expenditure rather than uncalibrated $g/\text{kg}$ additions.

### Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) Equations

Always account for biological sex when estimating BMR:

- **Mifflin-St Jeor Equation (Male)**:
  $$\text{BMR (male)} = 10 \times \text{Weight (kg)} + 6.25 \times \text{Height (cm)} - 5 \times \text{Age (years)} + 5$$
- **Mifflin-St Jeor Equation (Female)**:
  $$\text{BMR (female)} = 10 \times \text{Weight (kg)} + 6.25 \times \text{Height (cm)} - 5 \times \text{Age (years)} - 161$$
- **Cunningham Equation (Fat-Free Mass / Gender Neutral)**:
  $$\text{BMR} = 500 + 22 \times \text{FFM (kg)}$$

### Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE) Framework

$$\text{Rest TDEE} = \text{BMR} \times \text{NEAT Factor (1.20--1.35)}$$
$$\text{Training Day TDEE} = \text{Rest TDEE} + \text{Exercise Energy Expenditure (EEE)}$$

- **Exercise Energy Expenditure (EEE)**: Must be extracted from digital training platforms (e.g. `intervals.icu` workout energy / kilojoules, Garmin, Strava, power meters) or asked directly. On cycling workouts, $1\text{ kJ mechanical work} \approx 1\text{ kcal metabolic energy}$.
- **Rest Day Guardrail**: Daily intake on rest days must NEVER fall below $\text{Rest TDEE}$. Recommending severe arbitrary deficits (e.g., $1,350\text{ kcal/day}$ when Rest TDEE is $2,200\text{ kcal/day}$) induces Low Energy Availability (LEA) and impairs recovery.

## 2. Daily Carbohydrate Periodization Framework

Carbohydrate (CHO) requirements depend heavily on muscle glycogen depletion rates. Glycogen cost scales exponentially with exercise intensity (utilizing muscle glycogen rapidly above 75% $\text{VO}_2\text{max}$).

| Training Load / Phase | Session Characteristics | Daily CHO Target (g/kg body mass/day) | Primary Fuel Purpose |
| --------------------- | ----------------------- | ------------------------------------- | -------------------- |
| **Rest / Low Intensity** | Light active recovery, skill work, or rest days ($< 45\text{ min}$) | **3.0 – 5.0 g/kg** | Baseline glycogen maintenance, promote fat oxidation |
| **Moderate Intensity** | 1 hour/day endurance or moderate interval sessions | **5.0 – 7.0 g/kg** | Refuel daily glycogen stores for routine endurance training |
| **High Load / Volume** | 1.5 – 3 hours/day endurance or double sessions | **7.0 – 10.0 g/kg** | Support heavy volume, prevent chronic glycogen depletion |
| **Extreme Load / Event** | $> 4\text{ hours/day}$ ultra-endurance or multi-stage events | **10.0 – 12.0 g/kg** | Maximum glycogen saturation and rapid multi-day resynthesis |

---

## 3. Protein Requirements & Muscle Protein Synthesis (MPS)

### Total Daily Dosage

- **Endurance Athletes**: $1.6\text{--}1.8\text{ g/kg/day}$ (supports mitochondrial protein synthesis, tissue repair, and amino acid oxidation during long bouts).
- **Strength / Hypertrophy Athletes**: $1.6\text{--}2.2\text{ g/kg/day}$ (up to $2.4\text{ g/kg/day}$ during energetic deficits to preserve lean mass).

### Protein Timing & Distribution

- Spreading protein across 4–5 discrete meals every 3–4 hours produces superior 24-hour muscle protein synthesis compared to skewed protein intake.
- **Per-Meal Target**: $0.40\text{--}0.55\text{ g/kg/meal}$ (approx. 30–45g high-quality protein per meal).
- **Leucine Trigger**: Each protein dose should contain $\ge 3.0\text{g}$ of L-leucine to fully stimulate the mTORC1 pathway.
- **Pre-Sleep Protein**: Consuming 30–40g of slow-digesting protein (such as casein or dairy matrix) 30 minutes before sleep sustains overnight plasma amino acid availability and nocturnal MPS.

---

## 4. Dietary Fats for Hormonal & Metabolic Health

- **Baseline Requirement**: Dietary fat should provide 20–35% of total daily energy, with a hard minimum of **$1.0\text{ g/kg/day}$**.
- Essential fatty acids (Omega-3 EPA/DHA) support cellular membrane fluidity, reduce post-exercise muscular inflammation, and preserve cardiovascular health.
- Avoid chronic low-fat diets ($< 15\%\text{ of total calories}$), which compromise fat-soluble vitamin absorption (A, D, E, K) and depress testosterone and estrogen synthesis.

---

## 5. Meal-by-Meal Caloric & Macronutrient Allocation Framework

To ensure actionable execution, convert daily caloric and macro targets into a 4–5 meal schedule. Each meal must explicitly state Calories, Protein (g), Carbohydrates (g), and Fat (g):

1. **Meal 1 (Breakfast)**: ~20–25% daily TDEE | $0.4\text{--}0.5\text{ g/kg protein}$ | High-quality complex carbs & healthy fats.
2. **Meal 2 (Lunch)**: ~25–30% daily TDEE | $0.4\text{--}0.5\text{ g/kg protein}$ | Balanced macros tailored to afternoon training status.
3. **Meal 3 (Pre-Workout / Afternoon Snack)**: ~15–20% daily TDEE | Easily digestible carbs & moderate protein 1.5–2 hours pre-session.
4. **Meal 4 (Dinner / Post-Workout)**: ~25–30% daily TDEE | $0.4\text{--}0.5\text{ g/kg protein}$ | Glycogen resynthesis carbs & anti-inflammatory fats.
5. **Meal 5 (Pre-Sleep Recovery Snack)**: ~5–10% daily TDEE | 30–40g slow-digesting protein (casein/dairy) + low-GI carb.

## 6. "Train Low, Sleep Low" Periodization Tactics

Periodically training with reduced glycogen availability ("Train Low") can amplify mitochondrial biogenesis signaling (AMPK, p38 MAPK, PGC-1$\alpha$).

- **Sleep Low / Train Low Protocol**: Perform a high-intensity evening session to deplete glycogen, consume low-CHO post-workout meal, sleep, and perform a low-intensity Zone 2 session the next morning before breakfast.
- **Guardrails**: Keep "Train Low" sessions strictly in Zone 1/2 intensity; high-intensity interval sessions (Zone 4/5) must ALWAYS be performed with high carbohydrate availability ("Train High").

## 7. Weight Loss & Weight Gain Guidelines & Safety Guardrails

Body composition modifications (fat loss or muscle gain) must be periodized outside key competitive windows and managed within physiological safety boundaries to protect hormonal function, athletic performance, and systemic health.

### Weight Loss & Fat Loss Guardrails

- **Safe Weekly Rate of Weight Loss**: Target **$0.5\%\text{--}1.0\%$ of body mass per week** (equivalent to $\sim 0.5\text{--}1.0\text{ kg/week}$ or $1\text{--}2\text{ lbs/week}$ for average adults).
- **Caloric Deficit Cap**: Moderate deficit of **300–500 kcal/day** below Training TDEE.
- **Rest-Day & LEA Hard Floor**: Daily intake on rest days must **NEVER** drop below baseline $\text{Rest TDEE}$. Energy Availability (EA) must **NEVER** fall below **$30\text{ kcal/kg FFM/day}$** (which triggers Low Energy Availability and RED-S down-regulation of thyroid, sex hormones, and bone mineral density).
- **Protein Protection in Deficit**: Elevate protein intake to **$2.0\text{--}2.4\text{ g/kg/day}$** during caloric restriction to minimize fat-free mass (FFM) catabolism and support satiety.
- **Unrealistic/Unsafe Goal Detection**:
  - Flag and reject any athlete request seeking $> 1.0\%$ body mass loss per week or rapid mass reduction (e.g., attempting 5–10 kg weight loss in 1 month).
  - *Risk Communication*: Educate the athlete that rapid caloric restriction forces severe muscle wasting, metabolic adaptation (RMR reduction), immune suppression, increased stress fracture risk, and performance degradation.

### Weight Gain & Muscle Hypertrophy Guardrails

- **Safe Weekly Rate of Weight Gain**: Target **$0.25\%\text{--}0.5\%$ of body mass per week** ($\sim 0.25\text{--}0.5\text{ kg/week}$ or $\sim 0.5\text{--}1.5\text{ kg/month}$ for trained individuals) to maximize lean mass accretion and minimize adipose tissue gain.
- **Caloric Surplus Cap**: Moderate caloric surplus of **+250 to +500 kcal/day** (+10–15% over TDEE).
- **Protein & Hypertrophy Stimulus**: Pair surplus with structured resistance training and **$1.6\text{--}2.2\text{ g/kg/day}$** protein split into 4–5 daily feedings ($0.40\text{--}0.55\text{ g/kg/meal}$).
- **Unrealistic/Unsafe Goal Detection**:
  - Flag and reject any request claiming rapid muscle mass gain goals (e.g., gaining 5–10 kg of muscle in 1 month).
  - *Risk Communication*: Explain biological Muscle Protein Synthesis (MPS) limits in natural athletes. Clarify that aggressive surpluses ($> 750\text{--}1,000\text{ kcal/day}$) do not accelerate muscle synthesis and result primarily in fat mass storage, metabolic strain, and cardiovascular load.
